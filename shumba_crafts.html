Our Heritage

We're not selling wood.
We're selling Zimbabwean power.

Every piece starts with the lion. Shumba means strength — and we carve it into ironwood and jacaranda, using techniques passed down through generations of Zimbabwean artisans.

The tribal patterns in our logo aren't decoration. They're our identity. Heritage. Handcrafted. Zimbabwe. That's our differentiator — and it lives in every grain of wood we shape.

Carved by fire. Forged by hands. Every piece unique. Every piece signed.
